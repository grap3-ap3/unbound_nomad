Tour pack

Everything goes in a Kemi Moto rear trunk organizer (affiliate link). My helmet takes about half the space. The rest holds miscellaneous gear: camera gear when I'm recording, leftovers when I eat out. It stays mostly empty around town. I only fill it for road trips.

Left (low side) saddlebag

Everything goes in Kemi Moto saddlebag liners (affiliate link). This bag stays mostly empty so it can hold ride-specific or extra gear: chaps, rain suit and boots, or my coat or hoodie if the day warms up.

  • Why I chose it: It makes for a comfortable ride and looks nicer than the stock windshield.
