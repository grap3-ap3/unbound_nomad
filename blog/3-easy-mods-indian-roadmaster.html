3 Easy Mods for the Indian Roadmaster You Can Do in Your Driveway

Posted on Thu 19 November 2026 in Blog

New bike, first mods.

Three upgrades on my 2026 Indian Roadmaster Dark Horse, all done in one morning with basic tools: a short antenna, a purple Klockwerks Kolor Flare windshield, and HogWorkz angled adjustable highway pegs. None of them need a shop.

The three mods

Short antenna

It looks much cleaner than the stock whip and still picks up a good signal.

Klockwerks Kolor Flare windshield (purple)

It pushes air up and over you, reducing wind buffeting. It makes for a comfortable ride and looks nicer than the stock windshield.

HogWorkz highway pegs

They give you extra leg room on longer trips. I like the look, and the adjustable clamps make positioning quick and easy.
