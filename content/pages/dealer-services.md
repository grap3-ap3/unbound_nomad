*Real video for your dealership, finished and ready to post.*

---

## What this is

Most dealers know video matters and don't have the time to make it. I'm a rider who films and edits, and I make the videos so you don't have to.

I come to your dealership, film, and send you finished vertical videos made to fit your style. They are yours to post on your own channels.

## What you get each month

- **Four finished vertical videos.** Short, edited, and ready for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts.
- **Your own style.** We talk through how your dealership looks and sounds first, and the videos follow that, not a template.
- **A suggested caption for each video.** Written and ready to paste, so posting takes minutes.
- **A one-page monthly report.** What was made, what was posted, and what to try next.

## How it works

**1. One filming visit.** I come to your dealership and film what we planned together, such as bikes, people, and the shop.

**2. I edit.** I turn that footage into four videos for the month, each with a caption.

**3. You post.** The videos and captions are yours to use on your channels, on your schedule.

**4. A one-page report each month.** Short, plain, and easy to read.

## The terms

- **3-month start.** We begin with three months so there is time to find what works for your dealership.
- **Billed monthly.**
- **Travel by distance.** I'm based in Delaware, Ohio. How far I travel for the visit depends on where you are, and we sort that out in the first conversation.

## Who is behind it

The Unbound Nomad LLC is run by Phil Grimes. I ride, I film, and I edit all of it myself. When you talk to me, you're talking to the person who will be at your dealership with the camera.

---

## Talk to me about your dealership

Send a short note: your name, the dealership, your role, where you're located, and what you'd like video to do for you. I'll reply and we can talk it through.
