<p style="font-size:1rem;color:#555;font-style:italic;line-height:1.7;margin-bottom:2rem">She needs a name. These four are in the running - all Native American warrior women who refused every limit placed on them. I take delivery today. The right name will find me.</p>

<div style="border-left:4px solid #90a800;padding:1.25rem 1.25rem 1.25rem 1.5rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem;background:#f9f8fc">
  <p style="font-size:0.65rem;letter-spacing:0.16em;color:#90a800;text-transform:uppercase;font-weight:700;margin:0 0 0.35rem">Apache &middot; Chiricahua</p>
  <h2 style="font-family:Georgia,serif;font-size:2rem;font-weight:400;color:#1a0028;margin:0 0 0.2rem;line-height:1.1">Dahteste</h2>
  <p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:#90a800;font-style:italic;margin:0 0 0.75rem">The scout who crossed every border</p>
  <p style="font-size:0.95rem;color:#333;line-height:1.75;margin:0">She rode with Geronimo - not as a follower, but as a warrior and scout. Dahteste could read terrain and move through country that stopped everyone else cold. When they said you can't go there, she went.</p>
</div>

<div style="border-left:4px solid #90a800;padding:1.25rem 1.25rem 1.25rem 1.5rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem;background:#f9f8fc">
  <p style="font-size:0.65rem;letter-spacing:0.16em;color:#90a800;text-transform:uppercase;font-weight:700;margin:0 0 0.35rem">Blackfoot &middot; Piegan</p>
  <h2 style="font-family:Georgia,serif;font-size:2rem;font-weight:400;color:#1a0028;margin:0 0 0.2rem;line-height:1.1">Pitamakan</h2>
  <p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:#90a800;font-style:italic;margin:0 0 0.75rem">Running Eagle</p>
  <p style="font-size:0.95rem;color:#333;line-height:1.75;margin:0">She earned a warrior name that by tradition was only ever given to men. The Blackfoot called her Pitamakan - Running Eagle. She led raids, proved herself in battle, and they had no choice but to acknowledge what she was.</p>
</div>

<div style="border-left:4px solid #603090;padding:1.25rem 1.25rem 1.25rem 1.5rem;margin-bottom:1.5rem;background:#f9f8fc">
  <p style="font-size:0.65rem;letter-spacing:0.16em;color:#603090;text-transform:uppercase;font-weight:700;margin:0 0 0.35rem">Wampanoag</p>
  <h2 style="font-family:Georgia,serif;font-size:2rem;font-weight:400;color:#1a0028;margin:0 0 0.2rem;line-height:1.1">Wetamoo</h2>
  <p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:#603090;font-style:italic;margin:0 0 0.75rem">War chief. Not a chief's wife. A chief.</p>
  <p style="font-size:0.95rem;color:#333;line-height:1.75;margin:0">Squaw sachem - the title for someone who commands warriors. Wetamoo led people into conflict and did it without flinching. She walked into rooms and people adjusted. She didn't project authority - she just had it.</p>
</div>

<div style="border-left:4px solid #603090;padding:1.25rem 1.25rem 1.25rem 1.5rem;margin-bottom:2.5rem;background:#f9f8fc">

  <p style="font-size:0.95rem;color:#333;line-height:1.75;margin:0">A Tongva medicine woman who organized the 1785 uprising against the Spanish missions. Twenty-four years old. When they put her on trial, she told the governor directly: I came because I hate the missions. I came to fight you. No apology. Just the truth.</p>
