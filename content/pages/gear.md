## In my saddlebags

How I pack the Roadmaster in winter. Items marked (affiliate link) go to Amazon. Items with no link are things I already had, or built from other kits.

### Tour pack
Everything goes in a <a href="https://link.amazon/B03yh8KAb" rel="sponsored nofollow">Kemi Moto rear trunk organizer</a> (affiliate link). My helmet takes about half the space. - **Why I chose it:** It makes for a comfortable ride and looks nicer than the stock windshield.
